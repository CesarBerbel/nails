Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Helen Regiani Nails (Helen Regiani), nail design in Coimbra, Portugal.
Contact: helenregiani25@gmail.com · WhatsApp +351 914 930 793.

What data we collect and why

  • Anonymous browsing on the website — pages and sections viewed, options chosen in the studio (shape, colour, finish, skin tone), steps of “Design your nails”, where the visit came from (e.g. Instagram), device type, language and time zone. This helps us improve the website and understand which services and colours are most popular. The website does not store your IP address or use advertising cookies.
  • Google account (optional) — if you choose “Sign in with Google”, we receive your name, email and profile photo. They are only used so you can save up to 5 favourite designs (“My nails”) and see them on any device. We have no access to your password or any other data in your Google account.
  • Booking request — the booking form is not stored on the website: the message (name, service, date, design and notes) opens in your WhatsApp and is only sent if you send it. The website only records that a request was made, with the chosen service and design, without your name.

Legal basis

Our legitimate interest in improving the website and services (anonymous browsing), and your consent when you sign in with Google or send a booking. You can stop using your account at any time.

Who we share it with

Your data is not sold or shared for advertising. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in (servers in the European Union).
  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • Google — authentication, when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — only if you decide to send the booking message.

How long we keep it

  • Browsing data: for as long as it is useful for the website statistics; it is anonymous and you can ask for it to be deleted.
  • Account and saved designs: for as long as you keep the account, or until you ask us to delete them.

Storage in your browser

The website stores in your browser (localStorage) an anonymous visit identifier, whether you have seen the “Design your nails” invitation and the privacy notice, and — if you sign in with Google — your session. You can delete this data by clearing the site data in your browser.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to its processing. Just write to helenregiani25@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.pt).

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the last change is shown at the top of the page. If there is any difference between this translation and the Portuguese version, the Portuguese version prevails.
